The West Side Soccer **AYSO** Super Camp is an opportunity for any and all **AYSO** Volunteers to have local access to a broad variety of training. 2022 is our first year offering the Super Camp and we hope it will be bigger and better each year.  These are IN-PERSON classes and are open to all volunteers from any Region.

* * *

## 1st Annual West Side Soccer Super Camp: June 24th, 25th and 26th 2022 New York City

##### Location:

Trevor Day School West Side\
1 West 88th Street\
New York, NY\
[Click Here For Map](https://goo.gl/maps/iAkWV1yiDEB8DGxM6)

### How to Register

All classes are listed on eTrainU. To find the classes, search for West Side Soccer Super Camp.

Please be sure to complete all prerequisites prior to the course date.

### Sustenance

We will provide a light breakfast (coffee and such) on Saturday and Sunday mornings starting at 8:30.

Lunch will be provided for the all day classes as well as for those taking both morning and afternoon classes.

### Questions?

If you have any questions, please contact: [michael.titowsky@mdafny.com](mailto:michael.titowsky@mdafny.com)
