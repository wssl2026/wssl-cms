Referee Certification Classes

<div class="alert alert-success">

**COURSE PREREQUISITES\
\
Before enrolling for any class, AYSO requires that all volunteers have:**\
\
1) Registered on the [WSSL.org website](http://inleague.wssl.org/) under Player & Volunteer **Registration**. This includes players who want to also be a referee (player registration is not sufficient). Registration as a volunteer kicks off an annual background check for adult volunteers which is a legal requirement. Volunteers under 18 years of age do not need a background check; and, \
\
2) Completed the online **Safe Haven** course; and,\
\
3) Completed the online **CDC Concussion Awareness** courses. See below.\
\
4) Volunteers over 18 years old must also complete the **SafeSport** online course. Unlike Safe Haven, this is an annual requirement. Please visit [SafeSport](/volunteers/volunteers/safesport/) for more information and instructions on taking the class.

</div>

Next, pick the level of referee certificate that is relevant to your needs:\

**LEVEL 1 (Beginners):** The **8U Official** online course is the entry-level course for volunteers with little or no refereeing experience (including even talented players of the game!). You will be eligible to referee games in 6U, 7U and 8U after completing this course and the other requirements. We love it when coaches are also certified referees, and also welcome players and siblings who are over 10 years old.

Course Prerequisites: Please see the green box above.

The course is split into two components, both of which are mandatory:

a) 8U Official Online course (online at eTrainU). You can complete the online 8U Official course at any time, at your own pace. Here's how:

1. Login to the WSSL inLeague website USING YOUR OWN EMAIL ADDRESS and click on the mortarboard icon at the top right of the screen to go to eTrainU. Click on the green button "Login to eTrainU (Powered by eTrainU)."
2. Go to the Training Library and under "Refereeing" click on "View Courses."
3. Find the "AYSO - 8U Official" course and click "Enroll." The course will be moved to your "My Training" folder and should open automatically.
4. Once at the "AYSO - 8U Official" course, click "Start."
5. Complete the training at your own pace. It takes about an hour. Once you have completed and passed the quiz (75%+) you may click "Exit." You will receive an email to confirm completion.

b) Once you have completed the online component, attend a **40-minute "WSSL 8U Official seminar"** to cover some specifics of being a referee in WSSL. If you intend to take an in-person Regional Referee class very soon then you do NOT need to attend the seminar as well. The 8U Seminar is offered regularly either on Zoom or in person, usually at the start of each season. **LEVEL 3:** The **Intermediate Referee** class is strongly recommended for referees in 13U to 19U and is mandatory if you plan to be Center Referee in those divisions. The course includes in-depth modules on fouls and misconduct, advantage, offside and positioning. The class is aimed at experienced referees who have a strong understanding of the Laws of the Game.

Course Prerequisites: Significant practical experience at the Regional Referee level. A good knowledge of the Laws of the Game is assumed and not taught at this class but will be on the test. Hence some pre-course preparation is recommended to brush up on the IFAB Laws of the Game.

Where and When:\
Not currently scheduled in WSSL. The WSSL Intermediate level class is usually scheduled in February/March each year.\
Please check in eTrainU for Intermediate classes in other regions by clearing the Regions filter.

**LEVEL 4:** An **Advanced Referee** class is aimed at experienced referees who want to be ready to take on demanding games.

Course Prerequisites: Intermediate Referee Certification, a thorough knowledge of the Laws of the Game is assumed as well as significant accumulated practical refereeing experience. Please see the green box below.

Cost: No fee. Lunch included.

Where and When: Not currently scheduled in WSSL. Please check eTrainU for classes in other regions (clear the Regions filter).

### Teen Referees

Young people aged 10 years and over, including Core and Travel soccer players and their siblings, and are very welcome to attend referee classes. Please bear in mind that classes are quite intense and take approx. 3½ hours. WSSL awards community service credit for time in class and refereeing on the field, subject to approval by the school. The Teen Referee program welcomes new members and will make sure that you have fun as well as serving the league!

Volunteer registration for young people is a little different from that for parent volunteers. Go [here](/volunteers/referees/teen-referee/) for Teen Referee instructions.

### How to Register for a Referee Class

These are the steps that need to be completed to register for a course. Some of them only need to be done once, if they're done properly. Please try to do these in order. It will cut down on problems and having to repeat any of these steps.

1. Volunteer as a referee by logging into your [WSSL InLeague account](https://inleague.wssl.org/) and checking the box for Referee on your parent profile (if you have not done this already at registration)
2. On the WSSL InLeague website click on the mortarboard icon at the top right of the screen to go to eTrainU. Click on the green Login to eTrainU button.
3. Go to the Training Library and under "Safe Haven" click on "View Courses".
4. Complete the "AYSO's Safe Haven - Online" course.
5. Complete the "CDC Concussion Awareness - Online" course.
6. Click on "Training Events"
7. Select the class you want. Hint: In the "Regions" Filter, type "Region 611" (that's us!). You will see a list of all available in-person courses. Choose 'U8 Official' or 'Regional Referee Course", etc., as needed. Please note the course prerequisites explained on the "[How to Become a Referee](/volunteers/referees/new-referee/)" page.
8. Click on "Enroll". Review the details for the class (time and place and requirements).
9. Put the class on your personal calendar!

### Safe Haven, Concussion Awareness and Regional Referee Companion Courses Online

The above courses are offered online and can be taken at any time at your convenience.

1. Put the kettle on.
2. Go to eTrainU (see above).
3. Click on "Training Library."
4. Find the course you need: Safe Haven, CDC Concussion or Regional Referee Companion Courses Online.
5. Click the blue "ENROLL" button.
6. Make yourself a nice hot cup of tea or coffee and grab a biscuit or two.
7. When you are ready, on the next page click START to begin the course.
8. Pro Tip: take a screenshot of the completion screen / certificate and keep it safe in case the system does not update correctly.

For Safe Haven and Regional Referee Courses, all modules for each course must be taken for the course to be considered complete.

### SafeSport

All adults, 18 years or older, must complete Federally-mandated SafeSport training for adults involved in youth sports. If you have completed this training with another sports organization, your certificate is also valid for WSSL. Details are on the [SafeSport page](/volunteers/volunteers/safesport/).
