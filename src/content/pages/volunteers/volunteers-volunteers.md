### All Volunteers Must Register - It's easy!

All WSSL volunteers - board members, division heads, chief referees, coaches, referees, team managers, and other administrators - must be registered as such with AYSO national.

Some categories of volunteers (anyone that could have supervisory responsibility over children or who will have access to children's contact information) must submit to a background check to ensure both the safety of our players and volunteers and the integrity of our programs. Backgrounds are checked by Sterling Volunteers, a vendor hired by AYSO national, to ensure the safety of our children. Your information will not be shared with any third party, and there is no cost to you.

If the volunteer code you selected during registration requires a background check, you will receive an email message from the service that performs these checks with full instructions on how to proceed.

If you are having trouble with your volunteer registration, please contact our Child and Volunteer Protection Advocate at [cvpa@wssl.org](mailto:cvpa@wssl.org).
