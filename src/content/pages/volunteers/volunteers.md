WSSL is a 100% volunteer organization. Every parent is required to fulfill at least one volunteer job during the season. That may be a team role (like a coach, assistant coach or team manager), a divisional role (like a referee, assistant referee, or equipment manager) or a league-wide role (like a division head, chief referee, board member, registrar, treasurer, etc.).

Please go to [Volunteer Opportunities](/volunteers/volunteers/become-a-volunteer/) for more information.
