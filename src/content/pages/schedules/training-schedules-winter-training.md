<a id="winterreg"></a>Register For Winter Training (including March flex make-up sessions)

To register for a winter training session:
1) Log into your WSSL in-league account
2) Choose Events from the side menus
3) Click on Event List
4) Scroll to find the session you are looking for
5) Click the link to register<a id="coresched"></a>

<a id="Core"></a>Winter Core Schedule

<a id="Travel"></a>Winter 2024 Travel and Tournament Training Schedule

The 2024 Winter Training schedule for our Travel teams and older Tournament Teams will soon be linked below. All training will be held at the Nat Holnam Gymnasium at CCNY. We have contracted to have the facility to ourselves (just for WSSL) during this time. **The program will run from January 6 through March 3, 2024 between 9:00am-1:00pm on Saturdays and between 12:30pm-4:30pm on Sundays.** The schedule will include the time, court assignment and Red Bulls trainer for each team. Please contact your program's director(s) if you have any questions.

**BLACKOUT DATES:**

Each facility that we use has some black out dates relating to school activities and holidays. We have do our best to give each group a contiguous schedule, but there may be exceptions. If any, they are noted on the event sign up page and in the schedule. Please make a note of these dates. Some changes may involve substitute locations or they may involve a time change.<a id="make-uppolicy"></a>

**MAKE-UP POLICY:**

We do not offer any make up sessions. Please check for conflicts BEFORE registering for a winter session.
