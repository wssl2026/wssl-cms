The game schedules for WSSL's Red Bulls Travel Teams games are set by either the Westchester Youth Soccer League (WYSL) or EDP/NY Club Soccer League, including the EDP League Cup, depending on your team.

These schedules are available via the links below -- you will need to know your team's team name and birth year.
