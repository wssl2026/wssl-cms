**THE INFORMATION BELOW IS CURRENT ONLY FOR OUR SPRING 2026 SEASON.**

On Saturdays, we run a free shuttle bus to/from Fields 60 and 61 on Randall's Island. Please see schedule below and be on time. Buses will depart at the times stated. Advanced sign up is not necessary.

**Bus Stops**

> -   Upper West Side: Southside of 96th street in front of Symphony Vets @ 170 W 96th Street, between Amsterdam and Columbus.
> -   Upper East Side: Southeast (Randall's-bound) and Northeast (Manhattan-bound) corner of 96th St and Lexington by the corner of the playground.
> -   Randall's Island Fields 60/61.
>
