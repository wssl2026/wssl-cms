### Location

Broadway & W. 251st St, The Bronx. For a map of Van Cortlandt Park, click [here](/assets/legacy/Image/Fields/VCP_Parade_Ground_Map_FINAL.pdf).

### Equipment

8x24 movable goals with nets. If there are any equipment or safety concerns please contact the WSSL [Equipment Director](mailto:equipment@wssl.org) or [Safety Director](mailto:safety@wssl.org) as needed.

<div class="alert alert-danger">

**Don't forget the trash!**

</div>

Every team is responsible for cleaning up after their game. Beverage containers, snack wrappings and boxes, etc. should be placed in nearby trash receptacles. Don't forget your sweatshirts, etc. Following the final game, the closing teams should do a quick sweep of the field and make sure the trash has been disposed of properly.
