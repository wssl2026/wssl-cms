### Location

145th Street and Riverside Drive. This is a turf field and it is located inside [Riverbank State Park](https://parks.ny.gov/parks/93). Enter from Riverside Drive at either 145th Street.

This field has [specific rules](https://parks.ny.gov/documents/parks/RiverbankATHLETICSAthleticFieldTrack-RulesGoverningUse.pdf) as it is a STATE PARK and not a CITY PARK. Please be sure to review these and understand them.

### Equipment

The field is full size with permanent goals, but we are currently using it for 7v7 games and for those we use one set of 6x18 portable goals and sand bags. If you are assigned to play at this field, coaches should connect with their division head for further instructions..

<div class="alert alert-danger">

**Don't forget the trash!**

</div>

Every team is responsible for cleaning up after their game. There is no food allowed on the field. ONLY water is allowed.

Spectators must watch from the cement bleachers outside the track that surrounds the field. Any food, beverage containers, snack wrappings and boxes, etc. that would be at the bleachers, should be placed in nearby trash receptacles. Don't forget your sweatshirts, etc. Following the final game, the closing teams should do a quick sweep of the field and make sure the trash has been disposed of properly.
