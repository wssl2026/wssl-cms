**Location**

97th Street between 1st and 2nd Avenues.  This is a turf field and it is located in the playground inside the chain link fence. This field is used for for practices and small sided games.

**Equipment**

One set of 6x12 movable goals with nets. If there are any equipment or safety concerns please contact the WSSL [Equipment Director](mailto:equipment@wssl.org) or [Safety Director](mailto:safety@wssl.org) as needed.

**Don't forget the trash!**

Every team is responsible for cleaning up after their game. Beverage containers, snack wrappings and boxes, etc. should be placed in nearby trash receptacles. Don't forget your sweatshirts, etc. Following the final game, the closing teams should do a quick sweep of the field and make sure the trash has been disposed of properly.
