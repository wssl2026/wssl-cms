## **TRAVEL and TOURNAMENT TEAM TRYOUTS for Fall 2026 - Spring 2027 Season:**

**Tryouts will be conducted in a 3 phase process:**

- **PHASE I: March 14th-15th: Open Tryouts for External players & Core players considering tournament or travel**
- **PHASE II: April 18th: All current tournament players up through U12 and those invited back from Phase I**
- **PHASE III: April 25th: All current travel players, current U13+ TT players and those invited from Phase I & II**

Tournament Team eligibility applies only to Core players U9 - U12.
Players U13 and above may only try out for the Travel Program (no Tournament Teams for U8 or U13–U19).

- See links for our [Travel](/programs/travel-teams/)  and [Tournament](/programs/tournament-teams/) and/or our [Travel FAQ](/programs/travel-teams/travel-faq/).

- Want to know about the daily ins and outs of our travel program, please visit our [Push Page](https://sites.google.com/wssl.org/west-side-soccer-travel/home).

- Players interested in receiving general information about the **2026-2027 season or attending pre-tryout events** should complete the [WSSLTravel Inquiry Form](https://docs.google.com/forms/d/e/1FAIpQLSfSguwCt_bvmRm3EIMJqlwBwRzcszwsCy8Qh-0kA25Qw6Y1QA/viewform).

## **Ready to Register?  Start here:**

**Is your child a WSSL Core player or a player from outside of West Side with interest in either Tournament or Travel teams?**

- *\*Registration is required to attend the March tryouts. Please locate the correct age division and use the sign-up links below.*

- *Please note that if selected your child will be expected to attend subsequent tryouts for April 18th & April 25th, respectively*

**Girls interested in Travel or Tournament Team?**

- *(NEW) Open tryouts for girls outside of West Side or current Core players:  **[Register here](https://inleague.wssl.org/app/event-signup/35D14A3E-F36B-1410-8762-00FFFFFFFFFF)!***

**Are you a Current Tournament Team (TT) Player?**

- *You must attend the April 18th Tournament Team tryout.*

**Are you a Current TT Player interested in Travel?**

- U9 - U12 player must attend *April 18, and complete the Travel interest registration using this **[LINK](https://inleague.wssl.org/app/event-signup/04F7463E-F36B-1410-8762-00FFFFFFFFFF).***

- Current U12-U13+ TT players aging out of TT, automatically attend Phase III on April 25th AND complete Travel interest ***[LINK](https://inleague.wssl.org/app/event-signup/04F7463E-F36B-1410-8762-00FFFFFFFFFF).***

**Are you a Current Travel Player?**

- *You do **not** need to register. Simply attend the April 25th tryout.*
