#### **Fall 2026**

Registration for Fall 2026 will open on May 25, 2026, at 8:00am.

If you do not have access to the internet, or do not have a credit card or debit card with which to make payment online, please email commissioner@wssl.org for assistance.

### [SUBMIT PROOF OF AGE (CLICK HERE)](/registration/proof-of-age/)

Fee is $200 for Fall 2026 Registration until June 30, 2026 (and $300 starting July 1, 2026). Financial assistance is available. Please complete scholarship application in [English](https://docs.google.com/forms/d/e/1FAIpQLSffKEcUoIjsTstFMBYeJAqvJ6iC82ZN0HAuRrPnbRNDIiTTmQ/viewform?usp=sf_link) or [Spanish](https://docs.google.com/forms/d/e/1FAIpQLSdFMT3RfDdtHBwaVFX5QiBZ0_RkHZn-ydPz_paD62NNTw9duw/viewform?usp=sf_link).

Divisions are based on birth year. Select the [DIVISIONS](/programs/core/divisions/) subheading under Programs to see what division your child is in and to see where and when your child's division will play before registering. We do not accept registrations of college players and if you are registering for high school divisions you must be able to demonstrate you are in a valid high school program.

BEFORE YOU REGISTER, please make sure you have reviewed the following policies (click to review):

1. [Refund Policy](/registration/refund-policy/)
2. [Attendance Policy](/registration/policies-and-waivers/#Attendance)
3. [Team Formation Policy](/programs/core/team-formation/)

5. [Proof of Age](/registration/proof-of-age/)
6. [Parent Code of Conduct](/registration/code-of-conduct-parents/)
7. [Player Code of Conduct](/registration/player-code-of-conduct/)
8. [Suspension policy for player and coach misconduct (yellow and red cards)](/assets/legacy/Documents/MisconductSuspensions.pdf)

**Refunds for Fall 2026 will only be given if requested on or before July 15, 2026 (less a $30 non-refundable processing fee), or if your child is waitlisted and not placed on a team by week two of the season. Children are expected to be at all games and pre-game practices. Please consider conflicts and review our [REFUND POLICY](/registration/refund-policy/) before you register.**
