### How to Register for Development Academy:

If your child is registered to play core or travel with WSSL:

(1) Log into your WSSL in-league account and click on "Events."

(2) Scroll down or search for the development academy session desired.

(3) Click on that session and follow the registration instructions.

If your child is not registered to play with WSSL, email commissioner@wssl.org for instructions.
